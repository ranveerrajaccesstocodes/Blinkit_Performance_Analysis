# Blinkit Sales & Operations Dashboard

An end-to-end Power BI dashboard analyzing Blinkit's business data across a 6-page interactive report — covering sales performance, customer behavior, product/category trends, delivery operations, and regional performance.

---


## Project Overview

This project is a **6-page Power BI dashboard** built to analyze Blinkit's business performance — order trends, revenue, customer behavior, product/category performance, delivery efficiency, and regional distribution. It's designed to help business stakeholders make faster, data-driven decisions by consolidating raw operational data into clear, interactive visuals.

**Goal:** `["Identify key revenue drivers, delivery bottlenecks, and customer trends to support quick-commerce growth strategy."]`

---

## Tech Stack

| Component | Tool |
|---|---|
| Data Visualization | Power BI Desktop |
| Data Modeling | Power BI (Star Schema) |
| Data Transformation | Power Query (M language) |
| Calculations | DAX (Data Analysis Expressions) |

## Dashboard Pages

The report consists of **6 pages**, each focused on a specific business area:

### 1️⃣ Executive Overview
High-level KPIs — total orders, total revenue, average order value, active customers, and month-over-month growth trends at a glance.

### 2️⃣ Sales Performance
Revenue and order trends over time, sales by category/sub-category, top-performing products, and seasonal/monthly patterns.

### 3️⃣ Customer Analysis
Customer segmentation, order frequency, repeat vs. new customer split, customer lifetime value, and retention trends.

### 4️⃣ Product & Category Insights
Best- and worst-performing products/categories, inventory/stock trends, discount impact on sales, and category-wise contribution to revenue.

### 5️⃣ Delivery & Operations
Delivery time analysis, on-time vs. delayed delivery rates, rider/partner performance, and order fulfillment efficiency by area or time slot.

### 6️⃣ Regional / Geographic Analysis
City- and zone-wise performance, revenue and order distribution on a map view, and identification of high-growth vs. underperforming regions.
